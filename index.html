Trailer types

All decks are 102 in wide. Multiple pieces up to 174 in wide may ride on one truck, one behind another, if length, height, and weight limits allow. Nothing may sit beside an overwidth piece along the same length. The listed length is the lower deck on stepdecks; their 102 in upper deck is additional usable floor space with its own height limit. The 53 Flatbed, 53 Stepdeck and 53 Lo-Pro fill deck length before placing pieces beside one another. Stretch Step and Stretch RGN carry one oversized piece each. Reorder equipment to set priority.
